Mental Health & Well-being

Good mental health is important for every senior citizen.

😊 Stress Management

Practice deep breathing, meditation, walking and hobbies.

😔 Depression Awareness

If sadness continues for weeks, consult a mental health professional.

🧘 Meditation

Meditate for 15–20 minutes daily to improve mental wellness.

🚶 Exercise

Light exercise and walking can improve mood and overall health.

👨‍👩‍👧 Stay Connected

Talk regularly with family and friends to reduce loneliness.
