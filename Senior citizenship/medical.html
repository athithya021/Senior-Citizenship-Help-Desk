Medical Information

Find basic health information and emergency guidance.

🤒 Fever

Symptoms: High temperature, headache, body pain.

First Aid: Drink water, rest, consult a doctor if fever persists.

🩸 Diabetes

Symptoms: Frequent urination, thirst, tiredness.

Advice: Check blood sugar regularly and follow doctor's advice.

❤️ High Blood Pressure

Symptoms: Headache, dizziness (sometimes no symptoms).

Advice: Reduce salt, exercise regularly, monitor BP.

🌬 Asthma

Symptoms: Wheezing, cough, breathing difficulty.

Advice: Use inhaler and seek medical help if symptoms worsen.

💓 Heart Attack

Symptoms: Chest pain, sweating, pain in left arm.

Emergency: Call 108 or 112 immediately.

🚑 Emergency Numbers

Emergency: 112

Ambulance: 108

Health Helpline: 104

Support: 9952215218
